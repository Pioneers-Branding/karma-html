What is TMS?

A Different Way to
Treat Depression

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive treatment that uses focused magnetic pulses to stimulate underactive nerve cells in the prefrontal cortex — the part of the brain that regulates mood.

Antidepressants work through your bloodstream and affect your whole body. TMS works directly on the brain circuit itself, which is why it can help when medication hasn't — and why it doesn't cause weight gain, sexual side effects, or fatigue. At Karma TMS we use advanced Apollo TMS technology with EEG-guided PrTMS protocols, led by Dr. Keerthy Sunder.

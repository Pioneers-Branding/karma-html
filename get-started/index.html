A powerful magnetic coil placed near your scalp generates precisely targeted electromagnetic pulses. These are directed at the left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex — the brain region responsible for mood regulation that's often underactive in depression.

These pulses safely stimulate underactive neurons in the prefrontal cortex — without surgery, anesthesia, or systemic side effects. You remain awake and comfortable throughout each 19-minute session.

Over the course of treatment, the brain forms new, healthier neural pathways — rewiring itself for natural mood improvement and emotional resilience. This is the power of neuroplasticity at work.

Sustained remission rates post-treatment deliver long-lasting mental health benefits. Most patients experience relief for months to years after completing their treatment course.

TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation) is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive treatment that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate brain activity. It's an effective solution for treatment-resistant depression and anxiety for those who haven't found relief with standard medications.

A standard TMS session typically lasts about 19-20 minutes. It's an outpatient procedure, meaning you can return to your daily activities immediately after treatment without any downtime.

Most patients describe the sensation as a light tapping on the head. It is generally well-tolerated and not considered painful. You are awake and alert throughout the entire session.

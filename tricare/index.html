What is TMS?

A Breakthrough for
Veteran Mental Health

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive treatment that uses focused magnetic pulses to stimulate underactive nerve cells in the prefrontal cortex. For veterans and military families dealing with treatment-resistant depression, PTSD, anxiety, and OCD, TMS offers hope when medications have failed.

At Karma TMS, our team uses advanced Apollo TMS technology and EEG-guided PrTMS protocols, led by Dr. Keerthy Sunder with 15+ years of experience. We handle every step of your TRICARE authorization so you can focus entirely on healing.

Frequently Asked Questions

TMS uses focused magnetic pulses to stimulate brain areas responsible for mood regulation. A coil placed against your scalp delivers painless pulses. Over 36 sessions (6-8 weeks), neural pathways reactivate, leading to significant improvement. At Karma TMS, we use advanced Apollo TMS technology and EEG-guided brain mapping for personalized treatment.

Yes. TRICARE Prime, Select, Reserve Select, and Young Adult all cover TMS for treatment-resistant depression. We handle the entire authorization process. We also accept Medicare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, and Cigna.

TMS is very safe. The most common side effect is mild scalp tingling during sessions, which typically subsides after the first week. Unlike medications, TMS causes no weight gain, sexual dysfunction, nausea, or fatigue. You can drive and resume normal activities immediately.

Each session is approximately 20 minutes. The full course is 36 sessions over 6-8 weeks (5 per week, Monday-Friday). Many patients relax or listen to music during treatment. No recovery time needed.

Nationally, 60-70% of patients respond meaningfully and ~50% achieve complete remission. Karma TMS maintains an 85% improvement rate due to our advanced Apollo TMS technology, EEG-guided brain mapping, and personalized treatment protocols.

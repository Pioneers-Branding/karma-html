FAQ

Common Questions About Our Psychiatric Care

Answers to the most frequently asked questions from patients about our psychiatric services, treatment process, and what to expect.

Call for More Info
What happens during a psychiatric evaluation?
Your first visit is a comprehensive 60–90 minute session with Dr. Sunder. He'll conduct a thorough clinical interview, review your medical and psychiatric history, use validated screening tools, and develop an accurate diagnosis. You'll leave with a clear understanding of your condition and a personalized treatment plan.
What psychiatric conditions does Dr. Sunder treat?
Dr. Sunder specializes in depression (including treatment-resistant), anxiety disorders, OCD, PTSD, bipolar disorder, ADHD, and women's mood disorders including postpartum depression and PMDD. He treats both straightforward and complex cases.
Do you accept insurance for psychiatric visits?
Yes — we accept most major insurance plans including Medicare, TRICARE, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, and Cigna. Our team handles all insurance verification and prior authorization on your behalf.
What is TMS and when does a psychiatrist recommend it?
TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation) is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive brain stimulation treatment that Dr. Sunder may recommend when medication alone hasn't provided adequate relief. It uses magnetic pulses to stimulate underactive brain regions. Sessions are 19–20 minutes with no downtime.
How is medication management handled?
Dr. Sunder uses pharmacogenomic testing to understand how your body metabolizes medications — reducing trial-and-error. He'll prescribe the right medication at the right dose and schedule regular follow-ups to monitor effectiveness, adjust dosages, and manage any side effects.
Do you offer telepsychiatry / virtual appointments?
Yes — we offer secure, HIPAA-compliant telepsychiatry appointments throughout California. Virtual visits are available for initial psychiatric evaluations, medication management follow-ups, and ongoing care. It's the same thorough care with Dr. Sunder, from the comfort of your home.
How do I get started as a new psychiatric patient?
Simply call us at (760) 760-5675 or fill out the consultation form on this page. We'll verify your insurance, schedule your initial psychiatric evaluation with Dr. Sunder, and begin building your treatment plan — typically within days, not weeks.
